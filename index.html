What is Crowns?

🚀 Launching Spring 2026 — £12.49

Crowns is a fast-paced, strategic card game where players race to empty their hand using three tiers of numbered cards — Bronze (1–3), Silver (4–6) and Gold (7–9). Each card played must equal or beat the last number on the table. When the number reaches 9, one full round must pass before it resets. Simple to learn, tense to play.

If you can't play a card, either draw from the pile or gamble by rolling the die — roll a 1 and the number resets so you can play anything; roll a 6 and you miss your next turn; roll 2–5 and you draw two cards. Six event cards shake up every game:

⚡ Trade 🔍 Scout 🛡️ Shield 🥷 Thief 🪞 Mirror 🔀 Twist 🎲 Fate 👑 Crownfall

Once your hand is empty you enter endgame — roll the die each turn and roll a 6 to shout CROWN and win. Roll a 1 and you're back in the game. Designed in-house and printed in the UK on linen-textured cards. Every deck includes a custom die in a premium silk bag.
