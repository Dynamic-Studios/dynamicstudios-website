Our Journey So Far

Oct
2025

Founded

Five students at The Highcrest Academy launch Dynamic Game Studios through Young Enterprise, determined to create something people love.

Oct
2025

Crowns Created

After weeks of brainstorming and prototyping, Crowns takes shape—a strategic card game designed for quick, intense matches.

Oct
2025

Dragons Pitch Success 🏆

We pitched to Young Enterprise Dragons at Pipers Corner School and secured £50 in investment—validation that our vision works.

Dec
2025

Going Public

Launched social media channels and this website to build community and gather feedback from potential players.

Mar
2026

First Prototype & 11 Units

We built our first Crowns prototype and produced 11 game sets, each bundled with a custom die and a metal crown collectible.

Mar
2026

First Sale + Retail Talks

Sold our first unit at the Young Enterprise Eden Shopping Centre Trade Fair and began talks with local games shop Tabletop Republic about stocking Crowns.

2026

Launch Ahead

Finalising production, preparing marketing, and getting ready to put Crowns in your hands. Pre-orders coming soon.
