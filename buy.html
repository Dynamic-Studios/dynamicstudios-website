Crowns Card Game

Eight event cards shake up every game. Trade — swap one of your cards with a random one from an opponent. Scout — peek at the top two deck cards and secretly keep one. Shield — protect yourself by skipping a turn safely. Thief — look at an opponent's hand and steal a card with a value of 6 or lower. Mirror — match the previous card exactly, carrying the same number onto the next player. Twist — choose any two players (including yourself) to swap entire hands. Fate — all players roll; highest returns 1 card to the pile, lowest draws 2. Crownfall — target the previous player's move and halve the current number (rounded up).

About Crowns

Each player is dealt a hand of cards. On your turn, play a numbered card equal to or higher than the last card played. Bronze runs 1–3, Silver 4–6, Gold 7–9. Reach 9 and one full round passes before the number resets.

Can't play? Draw from the pile or gamble — roll a 1 and the number resets; roll a 6 and miss your next turn; roll 2–5 and draw two cards.
