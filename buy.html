Crowns – Premium Card Game

A fast-paced, 2–4 player card game of timing, tactics, and bold gambles. Climb from Bronze to Gold, unleash powerful Event cards, and be the first to empty your hand and roll a 6 to claim the Crown.

Launching Spring 2026 — limited first print run


What You’re Buying

Each player starts with a small hand of cards. On your turn, play a numbered card equal to or higher than the last card on the table. Bronze runs 1–3, Silver 4–6, Gold 7–9. Hit 9 and the table must survive one full round before the number resets.

If you can’t play, you’ll either draw or gamble with the custom die. Push your luck to reset the table, punish yourself with extra cards, or risk missing your next turn entirely.

Event Cards Included

Eight Event cards keep every game tense and unpredictable. Swap hands, steal cards, rewrite the current number, or force everyone to roll and suffer the consequences.

Empty your hand to enter endgame. From then on, you’re rolling each turn, racing to hit a 6 and shout CROWN before anyone else can finish and steal the win.
