About the Game

Crowns is a small-box card game designed for 2–4 players. Each round you race to empty your hand by playing cards in ascending order — but 8 Event cards, the gamble dice, and the CROWN finish line make every game unpredictable and replayable.

🎴
How it works

Play cards equal to or higher than the last. Bronze (1–3), Silver (4–6), Gold (7–9). Reach 9 and the table must survive one full round before the number resets.

⚡
Event Cards

Eight event cards — Trade, Scout, Shield, Thief, Mirror, Twist, Fate, and Crownfall — can reverse fortunes instantly. Play them on any turn once the first card is down.

🎲
The Gamble

Can't play a card? Draw one from the pile, or roll the dice and gamble. A 1 resets the number; a 6 costs you your next turn; 2–5 means drawing two cards.

👑
Claiming the Crown

Empty your hand and enter endgame. On each of your turns, roll the dice — roll a 6 and shout CROWN before anyone else does. That's your win.

♛ Game Rules

Crowns Classic — 2 to 4 players

🎯 Goal

Be the first to play all your cards, then roll a 6 and shout CROWN to win!

🃏 Setup

  • Shuffle all cards. Deal 5 cards face-down to each player.
  • Remaining cards go face-down in the centre as the draw pile.
  • Each player rolls the die — highest roll goes first.

🔄 On Your Turn

  • Play any numbered card to start. After that, each player must play a card equal to or higher than the last number played.
  • Play goes clockwise.
  • If the number reaches 9, one full round passes before it resets to 0.
  • Play an Event card on any turn (not as the very first card of the game).

⚠️ If You Can't Play

  • Pick Up — draw 1 card from the pile and pass, or
  • Gamble — roll the die: 1 = number resets, play any card · 6 = miss your next turn · 2–5 = draw 2 cards

👑 Endgame

Once your hand is empty, roll the die each turn. Roll a 6 and shout CROWN to win! Roll a 1 and you must pick up a card and re-enter the game.

🎴 Card Rarities

  • 🟫 Bronze — values 1, 2, 3
  • ⬜ Silver — values 4, 5, 6
  • 🟡 Gold — values 7, 8, 9
  • 🔵 Events — 8 cards (one of each)

⚡ Event Cards

Trade
Choose a player and swap one of your cards with a random one of theirs.
Scout
Look at the top 2 deck cards and secretly choose one to keep.
Shield
Protect yourself by skipping your next turn safely — no penalty.
Thief
Look at an opponent's hand and take one of their cards with a value of 6 or lower.
Mirror
Match the previous card exactly — the same number carries onto the next player.
Twist
Choose any two players (including yourself) to swap entire hands.
Fate
All players roll — highest returns 1 card to the pile; lowest draws 2.
Crownfall
Target the previous player's move and halve the current number (rounded up).
