How to Play

1 Player vs 2 Bots · Demo

🎯 Goal

Be first to empty your hand, then roll a 6 and shout CROWN!

🃏 On Your Turn

  • Play one card equal to or higher than the current number.
  • The turn automatically advances after you play.
  • If you can't play anything, the Pick Up Card and Gamble Roll buttons appear.

🎲 Forfeit Options (auto-shown)

  • Pick Up 1 Card — draw a card and pass your turn.
  • Gamble Roll — roll the die:
    🎯 1 resets the number · 😐 2–5 draw 2 cards · 😬 6 miss next turn

⚡ Event Cards (this demo)

Trade
Choose a player and swap one of your cards with a random one of theirs
Scout
Look at the top 2 deck cards and secretly choose one to keep
Shield
Skip your next turn safely — no penalty
Twist
Choose any two players (including yourself) and swap their entire hands
Fate
All players roll — highest returns 1 card to the pile, lowest draws 2
Crownfall
Halve the current number (rounds up if odd)

👑 Winning

  • Hand empty? Roll each turn — roll a 6 and shout CROWN to win.
  • Roll a 1 in endgame → pick up 1 card, re-enter the game.
  • Roll 2–5 in endgame → keep rolling next turn.
